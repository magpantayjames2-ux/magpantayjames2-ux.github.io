Arduino hardware projects

Two hands-on builds from my Computer Engineering degree. In both I wired the circuits, breadboarded the hardware and wrote the Arduino code.

Role Hardware and programmerPlatform Arduino

Project one

4-Bit ADC to DAC Converter

ArduinoBreadboardingADCDACElectronics

A circuit that reads an analog signal, converts it to a 4-bit digital value, and converts it back to analog. It demonstrates how digital systems sample and reconstruct real-world signals.

I built the full circuit on a breadboard, and programmed the Arduino to run it. Working at this level made quantization and resolution concrete, because the 4-bit steps are visible in the output.

Project two

Line Follower Robot

Arduino UnoIR SensorsMotor DriverRobotics

A small autonomous robot that follows a line on the ground. Infrared sensors detect the line, the Arduino Uno decides how to steer, and a motor driver controls the wheels.

I wired the sensors and motor driver, assembled the chassis electronics, and programmed the steering logic.
